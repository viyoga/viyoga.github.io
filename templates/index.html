$ dict desiderium
desiderium /ˌdɛsɪˈdɛriəm/ noun
DEF

An ardent, deeply felt longing or desire — most often for something lost, absent, or unattainable.

ETYM

Latin, from desiderare — literally “to long for the stars,” from de- “away” + sidus “star.”

EX

“In the quiet of the old house, he felt a gentle desiderium for a life he had left behind.”
